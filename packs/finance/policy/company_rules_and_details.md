## Section 2: Statutory & Corporate Compliance Rules

### CR-01 Buyer Entity and GSTIN Matching
Every invoice submitted for payment must explicitly name "Meridian Enterprises Pvt Ltd" or "Meridian Traders Pvt Ltd" as the Buyer with our registered GSTIN `27AAAPL1234F1Z8`. Invoices billed to third-party entities, sister concerns without inter-company agreements, or personal employee names are strictly rejected.

### CR-02 Active Vendor Master Verification
Invoices can only be processed for vendors registered in Meridian's Active Vendor Master. Vendors marked as Suspended, Blacklisted, Under Investigation, or Cancelled under GST Rule 29 must be immediately blocked from payment and reported to Risk Compliance.

### CR-03 Mathematical Precision and Reconciliation
Invoice calculations must reconcile with mathematical exactness:
`Sum of Line Items = Subtotal (Taxable Value)`
`Taxable Value + CGST + SGST + IGST = Grand Total`
A maximum rounding tolerance of ₹1.00 is permitted for fractional paise rounding. Any discrepancy exceeding ₹1.00 indicates billing fraud or accounting errors and must result in an immediate block.

### CR-04 Dual GST Split Integrity
Under the Indian Goods and Services Tax Act:
1. **Intra-State Transactions (Supplier in Maharashtra, State Code 27)**: Tax must be split into equal CGST (50%) and SGST (50%). IGST must be exactly ₹0.00.
2. **Inter-State Transactions (Supplier outside Maharashtra)**: 100% tax must be levied as IGST. CGST and SGST must be exactly ₹0.00.
Any invoice presenting unequal CGST and SGST on intra-state supply, or mixing CGST with IGST, violates statutory filing guidelines and is rejected.

### CR-05 Statutory GST Slabs
Only standard Indian GST rates are eligible for input tax credit: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Non-standard effective rates (such as 7.5%, 15%, etc.) are disallowed unless accompanied by a government gazette exemption certificate.

### CR-06 Tiered Spending Authority and Limits
Meridian enforces strict internal approval thresholds:
- **Tier 1 (Auto-Approval)**: Invoices up to ₹1,00,000 that pass all other checks are eligible for zero-touch straight-through portal submission.
- **Tier 2 (Manager Escalation)**: Invoices between ₹1,00,001 and ₹2,50,000 require secondary approval from the Department Head or Procurement Manager.
- **Tier 3 (Executive Prohibition)**: Invoices exceeding ₹2,50,000 must NOT be automated through portal bots and require dual wet-ink or digital token authorization from the VP of Finance or CFO.

### CR-07 Section 43B(h) MSME Statutory Compliance
Pursuant to Section 43B(h) of the Indian Income Tax Act:
All payments to micro and small enterprises (MSMEs with valid Udyam Registration numbers) must be settled within the statutory limit of 45 days (if an agreement exists) or 15 days (without agreement). Invoices nearing the 45-day window must be prioritized in the payment queue to prevent tax penalty disallowances.

### CR-08 E-Invoice QR Code and 64-Character IRN
As per CBIC e-invoicing mandates, B2B invoices issued by corporate suppliers with aggregate turnover exceeding statutory thresholds must carry a valid 64-hexadecimal character Invoice Reference Number (IRN) generated from the NIC E-Invoice Portal. Missing or invalid IRNs will lead to loss of Input Tax Credit (ITC).

### CR-09 Strict Anti-Duplicate Protection
An invoice number from the same supplier GSTIN can only be paid once. If an invoice with the identical vendor GSTIN and invoice number already exists in Meridian's paid claims ledger, it is flagged as a duplicate submission and blocked.

### CR-10 Invoice Freshness and Date Validity
Invoices dated in the future are fraudulent or premature and must be blocked. Invoices with billing dates older than 90 days from submission are classified as stale claims and require special CFO variance approval before processing.

### CR-11 Mandatory Purchase Order (PO) Binding
Every commercial invoice must reference a valid Meridian Purchase Order (e.g., PO-9001). Items billed must match approved procurement specifications and unit rates.
