# Meridian Traders Pvt Ltd - Vendor Payment Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

### VP-1.1 Scope
This SOP applies to all vendor invoices submitted through the Vendor Claim Portal.

### VP-2.1 Vendor eligibility
A vendor must hold an active GSTIN in the registry at the time of payment. Suspended or cancelled vendors are rejected.

### VP-2.2 GSTIN validity
A GSTIN must be 15 characters and pass the checksum. Invoices with an invalid GSTIN are returned to the vendor.

### VP-3.1 Invoice arithmetic
Line amounts, taxable value, taxes and total must reconcile within Rs 1.00.

### VP-3.2 Tax split
For intra-state supply (same state code) CGST and SGST are equal and IGST is nil. For inter-state supply only IGST applies.

### VP-3.3 Accepted rates
The effective GST rate on an invoice must be 5% or 18% unless the Finance Head grants an exception.

### VP-4.1 Auto-approval limit
Invoices up to Rs 1,00,000 may be auto-approved when every other check passes.

### VP-4.2 Manager approval
Invoices from Rs 1,00,001 to Rs 2,50,000 require manager approval before payment.

### VP-4.3 Finance Head approval
Invoices above Rs 2,50,000 must not be processed through the portal and are routed to the Finance Head.

### VP-5.1 Invoice age
Invoices older than 90 days are not payable without a Finance Head exception.

### VP-5.2 Future dates
Invoices dated in the future are rejected.

### VP-6.1 Duplicates
An invoice number from the same vendor GSTIN is paid once only.

### VP-7.1 Evidence
Every value used for payment must be traceable to the source document. Values that cannot be verified are escalated to a human.

### VP-7.2 Audit
Every automated action is logged with its inputs, checks and clause citations.
